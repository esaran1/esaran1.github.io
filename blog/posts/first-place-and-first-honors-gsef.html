GSEF Science Fair Award

First Place and 1st Honors at GSEF

Mar 2026 · 4 min read

I'm incredibly excited to share that I took 1st Place in my category and earned 1st Honors at this year's Georgia Science & Engineering Fair (GSEF).

GSEF brings together some of the most talented young scientists and engineers from across the state of Georgia, and competing among them was an experience I'll carry with me for a long time. Months of research, late nights, revisions, and a whole lot of learning all came together at the fair, and I couldn't be prouder of how it turned out.

"Science isn't about being the smartest person in the room. It's about being the most curious."

Walking away with both a category win and 1st Honors recognition was beyond what I had hoped for when I first started this project. It's a reminder that the hard work and dedication you pour into something genuinely shows. I'm deeply grateful to everyone who supported me along the way: mentors, teachers, family, and friends who believed in the project from day one.

More than the awards themselves, what I'll remember most is the energy of fair day. Seeing the incredible projects from other competitors, connecting with fellow students who share a passion for discovery, and feeling the excitement of presenting work that I truly care about.

A note on ISEF

While I'm thrilled with how GSEF went, I sadly did not qualify for the International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF) this year. It's disappointing, but it only makes me more motivated. I plan to use this time to strengthen my research and come back with an even stronger project next year.

I'm already looking forward to competing at GSEF again next year. This experience has only sharpened my drive to go deeper, ask harder questions, and push my research further. There's so much more I want to explore, and I can't wait to get started.

Here's to next year.
